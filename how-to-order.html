Simple ordering

From enquiry to your warehouse in 5 steps

No surprises: you approve the artwork, photos and specifications before bulk production starts, and see photos of your goods before they ship.

  1. 1

    Send your enquiry

    Tell us the model, quantity, colours, logo and destination country.

  2. 2

    Receive your quotation

    Tiered pricing for 300 / 500 / 1,000+ pcs, lead time and shipping options.

  3. 3

    Confirm artwork & specifications

    We send a digital logo proof, colour references and product photos or video for your sign-off before production.

  4. 4

    Production & pre-dispatch check

    Bulk production, then quantity, colour, print and packing checked with photos.

  5. 5

    Shipping & documents

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order quantity for custom-logo bottles?

300 pieces per model, with your logo included. The CB-04 telescopic bottle starts at 1,000 pieces per colour.

Can you match our brand colour and print our logo?

Yes. Choose stock colours or match your brand colour, split one order across several colours, and add logo printing. We prepare a free artwork proof for your approval.

How long does production take?

About 15 days for CB-01 and about 27–29 days for CB-02, CB-03 and CB-04, counted from artwork approval, plus transit time.

Are the bottles food-safe?

Every model is tested by an independent lab against US FDA 21 CFR food-contact rules. We share the full reports before you order.

Can I order samples first?

Yes. Samples are arranged on request and shipped directly from the production unit; charges apply.

Which countries do you ship to?

We ship by sea, air or express courier to India, the UAE, the USA and beyond, with the commercial invoice, packing list and other export documents.

Ready to launch bottles under your brand?

Tell us the model, quantity and colours — we reply with tiered pricing, lead time and shipping options.
