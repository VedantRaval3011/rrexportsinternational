Who we are

Your reliable sourcing partner for modern drinkware

R&R Exports International is a sourcing and export company based in Surendranagar, Gujarat, India. We work with carefully selected manufacturers to deliver retail-ready products under your brand.

We handle sampling, branding, quality checks, documents and shipping — one point of contact from enquiry to delivery.

R&R Exports International is also known as RR Exports, R and R Exports and RR International. We are based in Surendranagar, Gujarat, India, and export collapsible silicone water bottles: origami foldable, flip-straw, squeeze roll-up and telescopic sports bottles.
